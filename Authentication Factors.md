The 3 Types of "Authentication Factors"

[ 1. Something You KNOW ] + [ 2. Something You HAVE ] + [ 3. Something You ARE ]
       (Password/PIN)             (Phone/Security Key)           (Fingerprint/Face)

Something You KNOW: A password, PIN, or the answer to a secret question.

Something You HAVE: Your smartphone (receiving an SMS code or push notification) or a physical hardware key.

Something You ARE: Biometrics like your fingerprint, face scan, or iris scan.
